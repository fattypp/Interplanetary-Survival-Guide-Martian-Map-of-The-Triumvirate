Basemap: CTX orthomosaic (about 5–6 m/pixel native, shown near 20 m/pixel), covering 77.10–77.80°E, 18.10–18.75°N. Crater marks come from detect_craters.py (matched-filter proposals checked by a random-forest classifier; no human labels, so some false marks remain, mostly small ones on smooth plains). Overlay positions for the delta outline, channel, route and samples were traced by eye on this imagery and anchored to the published landing coordinates (18.4446°N, 77.4509°E). Treat them as approximate; sample positions are placed by named area, not by individual rover fix.
